Websites go stale. WhatsApp never does.

Owners already live in WhatsApp. Orders, photos, and prices are sent there every day, long before anyone opens a website editor.

Updating a site feels like extra work. So prices go out of date and sold out items stay listed, and customers stop trusting the page.

A developer is too slow and too costly. Waiting days to change one price is not an option for a small shop.

Three steps, and one of them is yours.

  1. Send a message

    Write to your Tuma number the way you would write to a customer. Add a photo if you have one.

  2. Tuma reads it

    It picks out the item, the price, and the stock, then replies to confirm what it understood.

  3. Your site updates

    The change goes live on your website. Nothing to log in to, nothing to publish.
